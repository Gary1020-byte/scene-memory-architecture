## What these four docs are for

Methodology docs make people "just follow along", but they rarely answer two questions:
**"Which one fits my situation?"** and **"Should this job go to a scene or to a sub-agent?"**

These four docs exist to answer them. Each gives you **criteria** (what to look at to decide),
not just **conclusions** (which one to use).

The first doc is the new main line: **sub-agents are not broken, they just cannot carry accumulation.**
Clear that up first, and the selection in the other three has a foundation.
